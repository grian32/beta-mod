The **Scorched Clay Ball** is obtained by breaking lava-adjacent Soul Sand with the Sifter enhancement.

## Obtaining

Soul Sand touching lava has a chance to drop two to four Scorched Clay Balls instead of itself when mined with a shovel carrying the [Sifter enhancement]({{< relref "/mechanics/item-enhancements.md" >}}#shovels).

## Usage

Scorched Clay Balls can be crafted into a [Scorched Clay Block]({{< relref "/blocks/scorched-clay-block.md" >}}), or smelted into a [Scorched Brick]({{< relref "/items/scorched-brick.md" >}}).
