**Resin** is obtained by chopping trees with the Resin Harvest enhancement.

## Obtaining

Naturally generated or sapling-grown logs have a chance to drop Resin when broken with an axe carrying the [Resin Harvest enhancement]({{< relref "/mechanics/item-enhancements.md" >}}#axes).

## Usage

Resin is used to upgrade an axe from Resin Harvest I to Resin Harvest II. It can also be crafted into a [Resin Block]({{< relref "/blocks/resin-block.md" >}}), used instead of a slimeball to craft a sticky piston, or used to craft a [Totem of Health]({{< relref "/items/totem-of-health.md" >}}).
