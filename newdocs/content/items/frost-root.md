**Frost Root** is a crop that grows only in Taiga and Tundra biomes. The harvested Frost Root can be cooked to restore two hearts.

## Obtaining

Frost Root Seeds can be found among an [Icy Dungeon's loot]({{< relref "/structures/icy-dungeon.md" >}}).
