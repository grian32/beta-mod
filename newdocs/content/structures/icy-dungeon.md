The **Icy Dungeon** is a rare underground structure that generates just below the surface in Taiga and Tundra biomes.

## Description

Icy Dungeons are built from two new blocks, Icy Stone and Icy Cobblestone, and contain various rewards.

[Frost Root Seeds]({{< relref "/items/frost-root.md" >}}) can be found among an Icy Dungeon's loot.
