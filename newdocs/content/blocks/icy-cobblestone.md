**Icy Cobblestone** is one of the two building blocks found in [Icy Dungeons]({{< relref "/structures/icy-dungeon.md" >}}).

## Obtaining

Icy Cobblestone is found throughout Icy Dungeons and drops when Icy Stone is mined. It can be smelted into Icy Stone.
