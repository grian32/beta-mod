**Icy Stone** is one of the two building blocks found in [Icy Dungeons]({{< relref "/structures/icy-dungeon.md" >}}).

## Obtaining

Icy Stone drops Icy Cobblestone when mined. It can also be obtained by smelting Icy Cobblestone.
