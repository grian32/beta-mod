The **Scorched Clay Block** is a decorative block that can also be used to upgrade Sifter to its final tier.

## Obtaining

### Crafting

{{< recipe id="scorched_clay_block" >}}

When broken normally, a Scorched Clay Block drops four Scorched Clay Balls. A shovel with Landscaper causes it to drop itself.
