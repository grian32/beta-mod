## Usage

The Sawmill converts planks to slabs at a 1:2 rate and to stairs at a 1:1 rate.

Configure the Sawmill by placing the desired output block—a wooden slab or wooden stair—against the side indicated by its texture. Once configured, right-click the Sawmill while holding a stack of planks to convert them into that output.

> The Sawmill converts the entire stack being held.
