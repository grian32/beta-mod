The **Moonwell** is a utility block that passively harvests moonlight to repair enhanced items during the night.

## Usage

### Repairing equipment

Right-click the Moonwell with an enhanced item to insert it. Right-click it again with an empty hand to retrieve the item.

A diamond tool at very low durability takes approximately two nights to repair fully. Tools made from lower-tier materials take less time.

The Moonwell repairs items twice as quickly beneath a broken moon.
