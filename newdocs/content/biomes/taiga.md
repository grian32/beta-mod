The **Taiga** is a cold vanilla biome modified by Project Beta Expanded. Its terrain remains familiar, but its vegetation and snow cover have been adjusted to give it a taller, more windswept appearance.

## Description

Spruce trees generated in the Taiga grow four blocks taller than their vanilla counterparts. Snow forms uneven drifts instead of covering every exposed block at a uniform depth.
