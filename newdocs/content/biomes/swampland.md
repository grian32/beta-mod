The **Swampland** is a vanilla biome fully reworked by Project Beta Expanded. Its terrain and vegetation have been reshaped to create a more distinctive wetland.

## Description

The Swampland biome has flatter terrain crossed by shallow pools and winding water channels. Crooked oak trees grow throughout the biome alongside sugar cane and Lily of the Lake.
