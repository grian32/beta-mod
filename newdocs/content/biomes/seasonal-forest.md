The **Seasonal Forest** is a vanilla biome modified by Project Beta Expanded. Its autumn foliage has been reworked to create more natural variation across the biome.

## Description

Seasonal Forest now has deterministic noise-based autumn leaf colors, along with an increased chance for large oak trees, pumpkins, and mushrooms.
