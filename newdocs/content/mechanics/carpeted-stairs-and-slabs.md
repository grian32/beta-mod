**Carpeted Stairs and Slabs** allow carpet to be laid over wooden stairs and slabs.

## Usage

Right-click a wooden stair or slab with any wool block to cover it with that color of carpet.

Break the stair or slab to remove the carpet. Both the wooden block and wool block are dropped.
