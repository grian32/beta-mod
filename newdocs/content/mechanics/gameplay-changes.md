Project Beta Expanded also makes a variety of smaller gameplay changes.

## Saddle Decrafting

A shapeless recipe allows a saddle to be decrafted into five leather.

{{< recipe id="saddle_decraft" >}}

## F3 Debug Menu

The F3 Debug Menu now displays the biome the player is standing in along with the world's seed.

## Fence Changes

Fences have smaller collision boxes, as seen in later versions.

Fences also no longer require a block underneath them when placed.

## Charcoal Texture

Charcoal now has a unique texture.

{{< item-image src="img/items/charcoal.png" alt="The unique Charcoal texture" caption="Charcoal no longer shares its texture with Coal." >}}

## Armor

Vanilla armor values have been changed to match Beta 1.8, and armor protection no longer degrades with durability.

## Boats

Boats have much faster acceleration and a higher maximum speed. They turn faster, cannot break from collisions, and drop a boat item when broken by the player.
