Project Beta Expanded applies the following fixes to vanilla:

- Wooden Stairs, Crafting Tables, and Wooden Fences now break faster with axes.
- Soul Sand now breaks faster with shovels.
- Cobblestone Stairs, Redstone Ore, Furnaces, and Bricks now break faster with pickaxes.
- Slabs only break faster with their respective tools: Stone, Sandstone, and Cobblestone Slabs use a pickaxe, while Wooden Slabs use an axe.
- The Grass Block item model now looks correct.
- Stairs now drop themselves.
- Code-defined recipes now work with stacks larger than one.
